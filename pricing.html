What a home safety assessment costs.

One flat fee, set by the size of the home and quoted before you book. No hourly billing, no add-ons, and nothing charged after the visit.

Prices shown include everything listed below. GST is added to every fee.

Fees

Priced by the size of the home.

A larger home takes longer to walk and produces more findings, so the fee follows floor area rather than a clock. You are told the exact price on your free call, before you commit to anything.

Included

What the fee covers.

The visit

A walk through every room you make available, inside and out. Photographs, laser measurements on every dimensional finding, and a conversation with you, the occupant or the family about how the home is actually used day to day.

The written report

Delivered by email as a PDF within three business days. Every finding carries a priority, a specific recommended fix, a cost range and, where relevant, a reference to the standard it draws on.

The plan and the contractors

A phased two-year plan grouped by what to do now, within three months, and within six to twelve months. Three screened local contractors matched to the work recommended, one per primary trade.

The follow-up call

Up to 30 minutes by phone or video, any time within 60 days of delivery, to walk through the report and answer whatever comes up once the family has read it.

Booking and payment

How booking works.

  1. You request a free call. We call back within one business day and talk through the situation.
  2. If an assessment makes sense, we email you the service agreement to sign online.
  3. Payment is due in full when you book, by Interac e-Transfer, or by cheque where arranged in advance. Credit cards and cash are not accepted.
  4. Once the agreement and payment are both in, we confirm your visit date and time by email.

Nothing is billed after the visit. A returned or declined payment carries a $35 administration charge, and the booking is not confirmed until payment clears.

Travel outside the standard area

Homes within 50 km of the Calgary city centre carry no travel charge. Beyond that, a travel surcharge applies and is shown in your agreement before you sign, never added afterwards. See service areas.

Comparison

What the fee buys that a free checklist does not.

Free home safety checklists are widely available and worth using. They are not the same product. A checklist asks whether a hazard is present. An assessment measures it, prices the fix, ranks it against everything else in the home, and names who can do the work.

A free checklistThis assessment
FindingsYes or no per itemMeasured, photographed, rated and priced
PriorityYou decideRanked by safety impact per dollar
CostNot addressedA cost range on every recommendation
FundingNot addressedPrograms that may apply, noted beside the findings
Who does the workYou find someoneThree screened local contractors, one per trade
