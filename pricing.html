What a home safety assessment costs.

One flat fee, set by the size of the home and quoted before you book. No hourly billing, no add-ons, and nothing charged after the visit.

Prices shown include everything listed below. GST is added to every fee.

Fees

Priced by the size of the home.

A larger home takes longer to walk and produces more findings, so the fee follows floor area rather than a clock. You see the exact price before you commit to anything, when you book online or on your free call.

Included

What the fee covers.

The visit

A walk through every room you make available, inside and out. Photographs, laser measurements on every dimensional finding, and a conversation with you, the occupant or the family about how the home is used day to day.

The written report

Delivered by email as a PDF within three business days. Every finding carries a priority, a specific recommended fix, a Calgary price and, where relevant, a reference to the standard it draws on.

The plan and hiring a contractor

A phased plan grouped by what to do now, within three months, and within six to twelve months. A Calgary price for every fix, from last month’s market research, and a checklist for hiring any contractor.

The follow-up call

Up to 30 minutes by phone or video, any time within 60 days of delivery, to walk through the report and answer whatever comes up once the family has read it.

Help paying for the changes

Many Calgary seniors qualify for programs that help pay for safety work at home: SHARP home equity loans and grants, RAMP for mobility access, AADL equipment cost-sharing, and the federal and Alberta home accessibility tax credits. Your report matches each recommended fix to the programs that may cover it, so you know where to apply. Each program decides who qualifies.

Booking and payment

How booking works.

Book online. Choose a time, enter the details the agreement needs, sign it on screen and pay by card. Your signed agreement, receipt and a link to manage your booking arrive by email straight away, and Carl calls within one business day.

Or start with a free call.

  1. You request a free call. We call back within one business day and talk through the situation.
  2. If an assessment makes sense, we email you the service agreement to sign online.
  3. Payment is due in full when you book, by Interac e-Transfer or by card through Stripe, your choice. Cash and cheques are not accepted.
  4. Once the agreement and payment are both in, we confirm your visit date and time by email.

Nothing is billed after the visit. The booking is not confirmed until payment clears.

Travel outside the standard area

Homes within 50 km of the Calgary city centre carry no travel charge. Beyond that, a travel surcharge applies and is shown in your agreement before you sign, never added afterwards. See service areas.

Comparison

What the fee buys that a free checklist does not.

Free home safety checklists are widely available and worth using. They are not the same product. A checklist asks whether a hazard is present. An assessment measures it, prices the fix, ranks it against everything else in the home, and identifies the trade each fix needs.

A free checklistThis assessment
FindingsYes or no per itemMeasured, photographed, rated and priced
PriorityYou decideRanked by safety impact per dollar
CostNot addressedA Calgary price on every recommendation
FundingNot addressedPrograms that may apply, noted beside the findings
Who does the workYou find someoneYou hire, with a Calgary price for every fix and a checklist for checking any contractor
