Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 1, 2026  ·  Last updated: May 1, 2026

Senior Home Safety is a service of Novo Industries Inc. We take your privacy seriously. We collect only what we need, use it only for the purposes stated here, and never sell or share your personal information with third parties for commercial purposes.

1. Who We Are

Senior Home Safety is operated by Novo Industries Inc., an Alberta corporation registered at Calgary, Alberta. Our service provides residential home safety assessments for seniors and their families. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information collected through our website (novoseniorsafety.com) and in the course of delivering our services.

This policy is intended to comply with Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) and applicable federal privacy legislation. For privacy inquiries, contact us at carl@novoseniorsafety.com.

2. What Information We Collect

We collect personal information in two ways: through our website contact form, and during the delivery of an assessment.

Through our website form, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address (optional)
  • The address of the home to be assessed
  • Information you voluntarily provide about the occupant's situation or concerns

During an assessment, we may also collect:

  • Photographs of the interior and exterior of the home
  • LiDAR measurements and floor plan data
  • Information about the occupant's mobility, health conditions, and daily routines, as shared by you or the occupant
  • Your signature on the service agreement

We do not collect sensitive personal information beyond what is necessary to perform the assessment and produce the report. Health and mobility information shared with us is used solely to make the assessment more relevant and accurate.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Contact you to schedule or confirm a call or assessment visit
  • Conduct the home safety assessment and produce your written report
  • Answer questions about your report or findings
  • Invoice you and record payment for services rendered
  • Comply with legal obligations if required

We do not use your information for marketing, advertising, or profiling purposes. We will not send you unsolicited commercial messages.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Formspree Inc.: Our website contact form is processed by Formspree (formspree.io), a third-party form service. Formspree receives and stores form submissions on our behalf. Their privacy policy is available at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, court order, or governmental authority.

No other third parties receive your personal information without your explicit consent.

5. Photographs

Photographs taken during the assessment are used solely to document findings in your written report. Photographs are stored securely and are not shared publicly or with any third party. You may request deletion of your photographs at any time by contacting us at carl@novoseniorsafety.com.

6. How Long We Keep Your Information

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide the service and for a reasonable period afterward in case you have questions about your report. As a general practice, client records are retained for seven years to comply with standard business record requirements. After that period, information is securely deleted.

You may request deletion of your personal information before this period ends, subject to any legal obligations we have to retain records.

7. How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable precautions to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or loss. These measures include password-protected storage of digital records, secure email practices, and limiting access to personal information to those who need it to provide the service.

No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights

Under Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Withdraw consent to the use of your personal information (subject to legal and contractual obligations)
  • Request deletion of your personal information where no legal obligation requires us to retain it

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at carl@novoseniorsafety.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Cookies and Analytics

Our website does not currently use cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics software that collects personally identifiable information. We do not use Google Analytics or similar services at this time.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of our website or services after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
