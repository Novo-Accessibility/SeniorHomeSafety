Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Who we are

Senior Home Safety is a service of Novo Industries Inc., an Alberta corporation (BN 716914023) based in Calgary, Alberta, Canada. In this policy, "we," "us," and "our" refer to Novo Industries Inc.

What information we collect

When you submit the contact form on our website, we collect the information you provide: your name, email address, phone number (if provided), the address of the home to be assessed, the occupant relationship, and any concerns you describe. We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry and schedule an assessment.

How we collect it

Our contact form is processed through Formspree, a third-party form processing service. Formspree transmits your form submission to our email. For Formspree's own privacy practices, please visit formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to respond to your inquiry, schedule and conduct your home safety assessment, prepare your written report, and follow up regarding your assessment. We do not use your information for marketing purposes unless you explicitly request ongoing communication.

Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share information with your occupational therapist, family member, or caregiver only if you specifically ask us to. We may share information with our insurance provider if required for claims purposes.

Assessment reports

The written assessment report we prepare contains detailed information about the home, including measurements, photographs, and a LiDAR floor plan. This report is shared only with the client who commissioned the assessment. We retain a copy for our professional records.

Cookies and tracking

Our website does not use cookies, analytics trackers, or any third-party tracking tools. We do not track your browsing behavior.

Data retention

We retain your contact information and assessment records for a minimum of seven years in accordance with professional best practices and applicable Alberta record-keeping requirements.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us at carl@novoaccessibility.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your information, please contact us at carl@novoaccessibility.com.
