What a fall risk check finds

Twelve categories, room by room. The hazards that cause falls are rarely the ones families expect — and they are almost never the expensive ones.

  • Bathrooms — grab bar placement, transfer clearance, tub and shower entry
  • Stairs and hallways — handrail height, tread depth, lighting at the top and bottom
  • Flooring and trip hazards — thresholds, rugs, transitions between rooms
  • Lighting and electrical — night paths, switch placement, cord runs
  • Entrances and exterior — steps, landings, winter approach
  • Kitchen, bedroom and living spaces — reach heights, seating, turning room
  • Memory and dementia safety — wandering, stove safety, orientation cues
  • Fire, emergency and personal safety — alarms, exits, call for help
