Where we assess.

Calgary and the communities around it, at the same flat price within 50 km of the city centre. Beyond that, still possible — just quoted before you book rather than added afterwards.

Beyond 50 km

Further out is welcome, and quoted in advance.

Homes more than 50 km from the Calgary city centre carry a travel surcharge. High River, Crossfield, Carstairs, Turner Valley, Black Diamond, Didsbury and similar communities all fall into this group.

The surcharge is shown in your agreement before you sign it and is never added afterwards. Ask on your free call and you will be told the exact figure for your address before you commit to anything.

Scheduling

A practical note on winter and distance.

Assessments run year-round. Exterior findings — step condition, handrails, drainage, approach lighting — are assessed in whatever conditions exist on the day, and the report says so where snow or ice limited what could be seen. Where an exterior assessment is materially affected, that is noted rather than quietly skipped.

If severe weather makes the drive unsafe for either of us, the visit is rescheduled at no charge to you. That falls under the no-fee category in the Terms of Service, alongside medical emergencies.
