Assessment Scope

Everything that matters.
Nothing left unchecked.

The assessment covers all 12 safety categories defined by the Senior Home Safety Specialist standard.

Entrances & Exterior

Steps, thresholds, pathways, lighting, and door hardware. The first and last places falls happen.

Bathroom Safety

Grab bar placement, tub and shower access, toilet height, wet-area flooring, and faucet controls.

Lighting & Electrical

Illumination levels, switch placement, nightlight coverage, and glare. Critical for low-vision safety.

Flooring & Trip Hazards

Rugs, transitions, surface grip, and clutter paths. The leading cause of home falls among seniors.

Bedroom & Living Spaces

Bed height, furniture clearances, cord management, and mobility aid navigation throughout the home.

Written Report & Capital Plan

Every finding prioritized by risk and cost, with a phased two-year plan showing what to fix now and what can wait.

Technology

LiDAR floor plans on every visit.

A tape measure is good enough for a renovation estimate. It is not good enough when you need to know whether a wheelchair clears a doorframe or a walker fits between the bed and the wall.

Professional-grade LiDAR scanning captures precise room dimensions, doorway widths, and clearance measurements far more accurately than a tape measure alone. It identifies problems a visual inspection would miss and creates a permanent record of the home's layout at the time of assessment.

Every assessment includes a full LiDAR floor plan with hazard overlay at no extra cost.

Funding Programs

Your report helps unlock money
that's already available.

Several government programs fund home safety modifications for seniors and people with disabilities. Our report gives you documentation to support the application process.

SHARP

Alberta

The Seniors Home Adaptation and Repair Program offers low-interest home equity loans and grants for home adaptations. Your assessment report provides the written cost estimates and modification details that SHARP requires with every application.

Learn more at Alberta.ca →

RAMP

Alberta

The Residential Access Modification Program provides grants for accessibility modifications. RAMP requires an assessment from an occupational therapist or physical therapist. Your SHS report gives the OT detailed environmental data and measurements from day one, supporting a faster and more thorough clinical assessment.

Learn more at Alberta.ca →

Home Accessibility Tax Credit

Federal (Nationwide)

The HATC provides a tax credit of 15% on up to $20,000 in eligible accessibility expenses. Your report helps identify which modifications qualify and provides the documentation the CRA requires for your tax return.

Learn more at Canada.ca →

About the Assessor

This work is personal.

I hold the Senior Home Safety Specialist (SHSS) designation and founded Novo Industries to bring genuinely rigorous home safety assessments to Calgary families. Not a checklist. Not a quick walkthrough. A real assessment that gives families the information they need to make smart decisions.

A close family member has lived with a mobility disability their entire life. I have seen firsthand what poorly designed spaces cost people: falls, lost independence, and daily frustration. That experience shapes every assessment I do.
