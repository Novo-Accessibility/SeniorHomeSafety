Questions families ask first.

Why isn’t the assessment free?

Free assessments are usually paid for by the renovation or equipment you’re sold afterward. We don’t sell renovations or equipment, and we take no contractor fees, so our advice is only about what’s safest for you. The fee is the whole business.

Can you check a quote I already have?

Yes. Book a normal visit and mention the quote. The report prices every fix on its own, so you can compare it line by line.

Can the report help me apply for government funding?

The report identifies which modifications may qualify under programs such as SHARP, AADL, the Home Accessibility Tax Credit and the Multigenerational Home Renovation Tax Credit. It is not an application. Where a program asks for a written cost estimate or receipts, those come from the contractor you hire; where a program requires a clinical assessment, that comes from a health professional.

Funding programs
How long does the visit take?

About an hour and a half for a condo or apartment, and up to three hours for a larger house. The fee is set by home size and covers the full visit however long it takes.

How soon is the report delivered?

The written report is delivered within three business days of the assessment visit, in PDF format.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

Cancel or move your visit for free up to 24 hours before it, and everything you paid is refunded. With less than 24 hours’ notice you can still move it once at no charge. If you cancel inside 24 hours, 10% of the fee plus GST is kept and the rest refunded. There is no charge for a medical emergency or hospital stay, severe weather, or a visit booked less than 24 hours ahead.

Is this an occupational therapy assessment or a home inspection?

No. It is not a clinical or occupational therapy assessment, a home inspection, a building code or accessibility certification, or a contractor quote. It works alongside your OT.
