Questions families ask first.

Who should consider this assessment?

Families and individuals who want a clear picture of the fall risks and safety gaps in a senior’s home. Most clients are adult children arranging an assessment for an aging parent, or seniors who want to understand what changes would help them stay safely in their home longer.

Does the assessment address Alzheimer’s or dementia-specific concerns?

Yes. The SHSS assessment standard includes a dedicated module on Alzheimer’s and dementia considerations: wandering risk, exit security, kitchen hazards for people with cognitive impairment, wayfinding cues, medication storage, and other environmental factors specific to cognitive decline.

Can the report help me apply for government funding?

The report identifies which modifications may qualify under programs such as SHARP, AADL, the Home Accessibility Tax Credit and the Multigenerational Home Renovation Tax Credit. It is not an application. Where a program asks for a written cost estimate or receipts, those come from the contractor you hire; where a program requires a clinical assessment, that comes from a health professional. Funding programs →

How long does a home safety assessment visit take?

About an hour and a half for a condo or apartment, and up to three hours for a larger house. The fee is set by home size and covers the full visit however long it takes.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

Cancel or move your visit for free up to 24 hours before it, and everything you paid is refunded. With less than 24 hours’ notice you can still move it once at no charge. If you cancel inside 24 hours, 10% of the fee plus GST is kept and the rest refunded. There is no charge for a medical emergency or hospital stay, severe weather, or a visit booked less than 24 hours ahead.

How soon is the report delivered?

The written report is delivered within three business days of the assessment visit, in PDF format.

Is this an occupational therapy assessment or a home inspection?

No. It is not a clinical or occupational therapy assessment, a home inspection, a building code or accessibility certification, or a contractor quote. It works alongside your OT. Assessment vs. an OT →
